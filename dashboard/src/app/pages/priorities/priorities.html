Normal

  • All active loads continue normally

  • Lamp works in automatic mode

Above {{ mockData.warningThreshold }}

  • Lower-priority loads are reduced first
  • Motor PWM may be decreased
  • Lamp remains protected
Critical

Above {{ mockData.criticalThreshold }}

  • Low-priority consumers are disabled
  • Only critical loads remain active
  • System enters protective mode
